FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most clients start seeing meaningful improvements within 2–3 months. Significant results typically appear by month 4–6. SEO is a long-term investment — the results compound over time and continue growing long after the initial work is done.

Do you offer custom packages?

Yes! If your project has specific requirements that don't fit a standard package, I'm happy to create a custom quote. Just reach out via the contact page with your details and I'll get back to you within 24 hours.

Is there a contract for monthly SEO?

I recommend a minimum 3-month commitment for SEO (to see real results), but there are no long-term lock-in contracts. After the initial period, you can continue month-to-month with 30 days notice to cancel.

What payment methods do you accept?

I accept bank transfer (SEPA), PayPal, and all major payment methods via Fiverr. For larger projects, a 50% deposit is required upfront with the remainder due on completion.

Do you offer refunds?

For web development projects, if I'm unable to deliver what was agreed, I'll refund your deposit. For ongoing SEO, you pay monthly and can cancel any time. I don't guarantee specific ranking positions — that depends on many external factors — but I do guarantee high-quality work and full transparency.
