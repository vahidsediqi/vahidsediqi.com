Why Shopify?

The World's Best eCommerce Platform — Done Right

Shopify powers over 4 million businesses worldwide and for good reason: it's fast, reliable, secure, and packed with features that help you sell more. I build Shopify stores that look premium, load instantly, and are designed with one goal in mind: converting visitors into customers. Every element — from the hero section to the product page layout to the checkout flow — is thought through from the buyer's perspective.

FAQ

Shopify Project Questions

How long does a Shopify store take to build?

Most Shopify stores are completed in 10–14 business days. The timeline depends on the number of products, design complexity, and how quickly you can provide brand assets (logo, photos, text). Rush delivery is available on request.

Do I need to provide my own content and photos?

You'll need to provide your product photos, logo, and brand guidelines. For copy (product descriptions, page text), I can help write or optimise the content — just mention this when you get in touch and we can discuss what's needed.

What's the monthly cost after the build?

After the one-time build fee, you'll only pay Shopify's monthly subscription (starting from ~€29/month) and any app fees for tools you use. My build fee is a one-time charge — there are no ongoing fees from me unless you choose to add my eCommerce SEO service.

Can you migrate my existing store to Shopify?

Yes, I handle Shopify migrations from WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, and other platforms. This includes migrating products, customers, and order history. Get in touch for a migration quote.
