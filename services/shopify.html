Why speed matters

A slow store loses customers before they buy.

53% of mobile users abandon a site that takes more than 3 seconds to load. A 1-second delay reduces conversions by 7%. Most Shopify themes are bloated with heavy scripts and unoptimised images that kill performance.

I build Shopify stores with performance as a first principle. Every section is hand-optimised, images are served in modern formats, and unnecessary scripts are eliminated. The result: stores that rank higher, convert better, and keep customers engaged.
