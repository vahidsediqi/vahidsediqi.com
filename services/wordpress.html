Why WordPress?

The Most Powerful Platform for Local Business Websites

WordPress powers over 43% of all websites on the internet — and for good reason. It combines unmatched flexibility with ease of use, making it the perfect platform for local businesses that want a professional online presence without paying ongoing platform fees.

A well-built WordPress website does three things simultaneously: it looks professional and builds trust, it ranks well on Google and brings in local search traffic, and it makes it easy for visitors to get in touch, book an appointment, or make an enquiry. These three elements together are what turn a website from a digital brochure into a genuine business asset.

I build WordPress websites from the ground up — no cookie-cutter templates, no bloated page builders that slow everything down. Every website I build is custom to your brand, optimised for the search terms your local customers are using, and set up so you can easily manage your own content after handover.

From a single-page restaurant site to a full multi-location business website — I've built them all. The €750 package covers most local business needs, and I'm always happy to discuss custom requirements.

FAQ

WordPress Website Questions

How long will my website take to build?

Most local business WordPress sites are completed in 8–12 business days from when I have all your content and assets. A faster 5–7 day rush delivery is available for an additional fee. The timeline also depends on how quickly you can review and provide feedback during the process.

Do I need hosting? What does it cost?

You'll need a hosting plan and domain name. I recommend reliable German hosting providers that cost between €5–15/month depending on the plan. I can help you set up hosting and the domain, or work with existing hosting you already have. This cost is separate from my build fee.

Can I update the website myself after it's built?

Absolutely. One of WordPress's biggest advantages is how easy it is to manage. After the training session, you'll be comfortable adding blog posts, updating service details, changing photos, and making other content changes yourself — no developer needed.

Can you add Local SEO management after the build?

Yes! Many clients start with the WordPress build and then add my Local SEO service (€350/month) to drive ongoing traffic to the site. The two services work perfectly together — a well-built website combined with active Local SEO management is the most powerful combination for local businesses.
