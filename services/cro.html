The CRO Advantage

Double Your Revenue Without Doubling Your Budget

Most businesses focus entirely on getting more traffic — more ads, more SEO, more social media. But they overlook a much more powerful lever: converting the visitors they already have. If your website converts 1% of visitors and you can improve that to 2%, you've doubled your revenue without spending a single extra euro on marketing.

CRO is the science and art of understanding why visitors leave without converting — and systematically fixing those reasons. It involves deep data analysis, user research, heatmaps, session recordings, and rigorous A/B testing. Every change is based on data, not guesswork.

I've helped eCommerce stores go from 1.1% conversion rates to over 3.5% — more than tripling their revenue from the same traffic. I've helped local businesses reduce their contact form abandonment by 60%. These aren't marketing claims — they're the result of methodical, data-driven optimisation work.

CRO works on any kind of website: eCommerce stores, service businesses, SaaS, lead generation sites, and more. Wherever you have traffic that isn't converting as well as it should, there's an opportunity to improve.

CRO Services

What's Included in My CRO Service

Heatmap & Session Analysis

I install heatmaps and record real visitor sessions using Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity. This reveals exactly where visitors click, scroll, and drop off — with no guesswork.

Funnel Analysis

Map and analyse every step of your conversion funnel — from landing page to checkout. Identify the exact point where most people leave and why.

A/B Testing

Run structured A/B tests on headlines, CTAs, page layouts, images, and pricing presentation. Every winner is proven by data before being permanently implemented.

UX & Copy Optimisation

Fix confusing navigation, unclear calls-to-action, weak headlines, and trust barriers. Good UX and persuasive copy are the foundation of a high-converting website.

Checkout Optimisation

For eCommerce stores, the checkout is where the most money is lost. I reduce cart abandonment with a streamlined, trust-building checkout experience.

Page Speed for Conversion

A 1-second delay in page load can reduce conversions by 7%. I optimise your pages for maximum speed because fast pages simply convert better — always.

FAQ

CRO Questions Answered

How much traffic do I need for CRO to work?

For A/B testing to produce statistically significant results, you ideally need at least 1,000 monthly visitors and 100 monthly conversions. For sites with less traffic, I focus on qualitative methods: heatmaps, session recordings, and UX best practices that deliver quick wins without needing large sample sizes.

How long does a CRO project take?

An initial CRO audit and implementation of quick wins typically takes 4–6 weeks. Ongoing CRO optimisation is a continuous process — the best results come from consistently running tests and implementing winners over 3–6+ months.

What kind of lift can I expect?

Results vary depending on how optimised the site already is, the traffic quality, and the type of business. Typical improvements range from 20–300% increase in conversion rate. Even a 0.5% improvement on a store with 10,000 monthly visitors and a €50 average order value is worth thousands per month.

Does CRO work for service businesses (not just eCommerce)?

Absolutely. CRO applies to any website with a goal: form submissions, phone calls, email sign-ups, booking requests, or purchases. If visitors are arriving at your site but not taking the action you want, CRO can help.
