Why organic matters

Paid ads stop the moment you stop paying.

eCommerce SEO targets buyers who are ready to purchase — they searched for exactly what you sell. These visitors convert at 4–6x the rate of social media traffic. And unlike paid ads, organic rankings keep working every day without ongoing spend.
