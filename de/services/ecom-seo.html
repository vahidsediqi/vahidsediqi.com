Why eCommerce SEO?

Stop Paying for Every Visitor — Earn Them Instead

Running an online store without SEO is like having a shop with no sign on the street. You might have the best products in the world, but if people can't find you when they're searching on Google, those sales go to your competitors.

eCommerce SEO is one of the highest-ROI investments you can make for your store. Unlike Google Ads, where you pay for every single click and the traffic stops the moment your budget runs out, organic search traffic is free and compounds over time. The rankings you build today continue generating sales for years.

I specialise in eCommerce SEO for Shopify and WooCommerce stores. I've helped online retailers go from virtually zero organic traffic to thousands of qualified visitors pro Monat — visitors who are actively searching for exactly what you're selling.

My service also covers Google Merchant Center feed fixes, which is critical for stores running Google Shopping campaigns. Disapproved products mean lost impressions and lost sales.

What's Included

Full eCommerce SEO Management

Product Page SEO

Optimise every product page with keyword-rich titles, meta descriptions, unique descriptions, structured data, and internal linking — turning product pages into ranking machines.

Category Page SEO

Category pages are often the biggest traffic opportunity for eCommerce stores. I optimise each category with proper content, faceted navigation, and canonical tags.

Technical eCommerce SEO

Fix duplicate content issues, crawl budget waste, site speed, Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, and all other technical factors that hold eCommerce stores back.

Google Merchant Feed Fix

Fix disapproved products, GTIN errors, image issues, and policy violations. Get your products fully approved and running at maximum performance in Google Shopping.

Schema Markup

FAQ

eCommerce SEO Questions

Does the €550/month package cover all my products?

The standard package covers up to 10 active products pro Monat plus all category pages and technical work. For larger catalogues, I offer custom pricing. The Merchant Feed Fix service (€350 per 100 products) is a separate one-time service for fixing disapproved products.

Which platforms do you work with?

I work primarily with Shopify and WooCommerce/WordPress stores. I also have experience with BigCommerce and custom-built stores. If you're not sure whether your platform is supported, just get in touch.

What is Google Merchant Center feed fixing?

Google Merchant Center is the platform that powers Google Shopping ads. When products are disapproved, they don't appear in Shopping results — costing you impressions and revenue. I identify every disapproval reason, fix the root cause in the product feed, and get every product approved and running.

How long until my store sees more organic traffic?

For established stores with existing traffic, improvements often show within 4–8 weeks. For newer stores starting from scratch, it typically takes 3–6 months to build meaningful organic traffic. The results then compound every month from there.
