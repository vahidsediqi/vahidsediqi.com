Why Local SEO?

Your Customers Are Searching Locally — Are You Visible?

Every day, millions of people search for businesses "near me" on Google. They search for "restaurant near me", "dentist in Berlin", or "plumber Hamburg". These are buyers who are ready to visit and spend money — right now.

The Google Maps 3-Pack (the three business listings that appear on most local searches) captures the majority of clicks. If your business isn't in that top 3, you are invisible to the majority of potential customers in your area. My Local SEO service is specifically designed to get you there — and keep you there.

I've helped restaurants, dentists, lawyers, plumbers, shops, and hundreds of other local businesses rank at the top of Google Maps in their city. The results are consistent, measurable, and life-changing for small business owners.

What's Included

Everything Your Local Business Needs to Rank

Google Business Profile

Full optimisation of your Google Business Profile — the most important factor for local rankings. Categories, attributes, posts, photos, Q&A, and regular updates.

Local Citations

Build and clean up your business listings across 50+ local directories. Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across the web is a key local ranking signal.

FAQ

Local SEO Questions Answered

How long until I see results from Local SEO?

Most clients see noticeable improvements in their Google Maps ranking within 4–8 weeks. Significant results typically come in months 2–4. Local SEO tends to move faster than general SEO because competition is more contained to a geographic area.

Do I need a website for Local SEO?

A website strengthens your local SEO significantly, but even without one I can improve your Google Business Profile rankings. However, for the best results, a well-optimised WordPress website or Shopify store combined with a strong GBP is the winning combination.

What's included in the €350/month Local SEO package?

Google Business Profile optimisation and management, local keyword targeting, citation building and cleanup, on-page SEO for up to 10 website pages, review strategy, competitor monitoring, and monthly reporting with a strategy call.

Can you help businesses with multiple locations?

Yes! Multi-location businesses require a slightly different strategy. I create individual location pages and optimise separate GBP profiles for each location. Get in touch for a custom multi-location quote.
