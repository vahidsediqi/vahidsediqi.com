Shopify vs WooCommerce: Which Is Really Cheaper?

The most common mistake people make when comparing Shopify and WooCommerce is only looking at the headline price. Shopify starts at €29 pro Monat, and WooCommerce is technically free. But the real cost picture is very different once you add up hosting, plugins, themes, developer time, and transaction fees.

WooCommerce can look cheaper on the surface — especially if you use shared hosting at €8/month and free plugins. But for most growing stores, you will quickly need managed WordPress hosting (€30–€60/month), paid plugins for payment gateways, shipping, reviews, email marketing, and potentially a developer to keep everything updated and secure. These costs add up fast.

Shopify is all-inclusive. Your plan price covers hosting, SSL, CDN, uptime, and security. You do not need to worry about server maintenance, WordPress core updates, or plugin conflicts. For founders who want to focus on selling — not managing servers — Shopify is often the smarter investment, even if it appears more expensive at first glance.

The verdict: For small stores with tight budgets and technical knowledge, WooCommerce can save money. For medium and large stores, or founders without technical skills, Shopify typically delivers better value when you factor in the time cost of managing a WordPress/WooCommerce stack. Use the calculator above to see which works for your specific situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is WooCommerce really free?

WooCommerce the plugin is free to install, but running a WooCommerce store is not free. You still need to pay for web hosting (€8–€60/month), a domain name (€10–€15/year), and most professional themes and extensions are paid. Once you add up all the costs, WooCommerce is often comparable in price to Shopify — and sometimes more expensive, especially when you factor in developer time for maintenance.

What is the cheapest way to run a Shopify store?

The cheapest way to run a Shopify store is to use the Basis plan (€29/month), use Shopify's own free themes, minimise paid apps, and enable Shopify Payments to avoid the extra transaction fee. With this setup, your total cost is around €30–€50/month including a domain — making it very competitive with a basic WooCommerce setup.

Which platform is better for SEO?

WooCommerce has a slight technical SEO advantage because it gives you full control over your URL structure, site code, and server settings. Shopify has some limitations, such as forced URL patterns for products and collections. However, for most stores, the difference in practice is minimal. Both platforms can rank well with the right SEO strategy. The bigger SEO factor is content quality and link building, not the platform.

Can I switch from WooCommerce to Shopify later?

Ja, you can migrate from WooCommerce to Shopify. The migration involves moving your products, customers, and orders. There are dedicated tools and services for this, including Shopify's own importer. URL redirects need to be carefully managed to avoid losing SEO rankings during the migration. It is a manageable process but does require planning and technical attention.

What are Shopify's transaction fees?

If you use Shopify Payments as your payment processor, Shopify charges zero extra transaction fees. If you use a third-party payment gateway (like PayPal or Stripe independently), Shopify charges an additional fee: 2% on Basis, 1% on Shopify, 0.5% on Advanced. This is an important cost to factor in — if your store does €10,000/month in revenue and you're on the Basis plan with a third-party gateway, you pay an extra €200/month in transaction fees alone.
