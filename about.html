My story

Started with a slow website. Stayed for the growth.

I started in digital marketing when I was frustrated by a client’s site that was losing thousands of euros a month simply because it was too slow and invisible on Google. Fixing that turned a $500 budget into $12,000 in monthly organic revenue — and I was hooked.

Since then I’ve worked with restaurants, eCommerce brands, SaaS companies and local service businesses across Europe and beyond — building the technical and strategic foundations that make search engines and customers trust a site.

I work at the intersection of code and marketing — writing clean HTML and structured data in the morning and analysing keyword gaps in the afternoon. That dual focus is why my results last.
