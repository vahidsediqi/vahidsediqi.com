My Story

Vahid Sediqi Helps Businesses Win Online

Hi, I'm Vahid Sediqi — a full-stack digital marketing and growth engineer based in Germany. For over 7 years, I've been helping businesses of all sizes build a powerful online presence, attract more customers, and grow their revenue.

My journey began with a deep love for understanding how search engines work. I was fascinated by the idea that the right combination of strategy and technical knowledge could take a website from invisible to dominating the first page of Google. That curiosity turned into a career, and that career turned into a passion for delivering real, measurable results.

Over the years, I've expanded my expertise beyond SEO to include full web development on Shopify and WordPress, conversion rate optimisation, and Google Ads management. This combination of skills is rare — and it's what makes me uniquely effective. I can see the full picture of your online presence and fix problems at every level, from technical code to marketing copy.

Based in Germany and working globally, I've had the privilege of helping restaurants rank on Google Maps, eCommerce brands triple their organic traffic, and local businesses go from zero online presence to market leader in their area.

My Philosophy

Digital Marketing Should Deliver ROI

I've seen too many businesses waste money on digital marketing that produces beautiful reports but no real results. Impressive-looking dashboards full of vanity metrics that don't translate into more customers or more revenue.

My philosophy is simple: every euro spent on marketing should come back multiplied. Every strategy I create starts with this question: "How does this make money for my client?" If I can't answer that clearly, I don't do it.

This results-first thinking is backed by a technical foundation that most marketers simply don't have. I can audit a website's code, fix speed issues, implement structured data, and optimise for Core Web Vitals — all while building the content and link strategy that drives rankings.

When you work with me, you don't just get a freelancer — you get a growth partner who is genuinely invested in your success. Your wins are my wins. Your growth is my portfolio. That's the standard I hold myself to every single day.

What I Love

What I Avoid
