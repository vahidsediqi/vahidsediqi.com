How to Use This SEO ROI Calculator

This calculator helps you answer the most important question in digital marketing. Once you’ve seen the numbers, explore our SEO service or use the On-Page SEO Checker to audit your site. This calculator helps you answer the most important question in digital marketing: "Is SEO worth it for my business?" The answer depends entirely on your specific situation — your current traffic levels, how well your website converts visitors, and how much each customer is worth to you.

Enter your current monthly organic visitors from Google Analytics or Google Search Console. If you don't know this number, a typical small business gets between 200 and 2,000 organic visits per month from local searches. Enter your conversion rate — for most service businesses this is between 1% and 5%. For eCommerce stores, 1–3% is typical. Enter your average order value or the average value of a lead to your business.

The calculator then shows you exactly how much extra revenue your business generates from organic traffic growth of 50%, 100%, 200%, and 300%. A 100% traffic increase means doubling your organic visitors — this is a realistic target within 6–12 months of professional SEO work for most businesses starting from a low baseline.

If you enable the SEO investment toggle, the calculator also shows you the annual ROI percentage and the breakeven point — the month at which your cumulative SEO returns exceed the total investment you've made. For most businesses, this breakeven arrives within 4–8 months, after which every euro of SEO traffic is pure profit.

What Is a Good SEO ROI?

A good SEO ROI is generally considered to be anything above 200% annually — meaning you get back more than three times what you invest. In practice, well-executed SEO campaigns regularly achieve 500–1,000%+ ROI over a 12–24 month period, because the traffic compounds and the cost stays fixed even as rankings improve.

Compare this to Google Ads, where you typically pay €1–€5 per click. Use our Google Ads budget calculator to compare costs directly, and the moment your ad budget runs out, all your traffic disappears. With SEO, the traffic you build today continues to arrive next month, next year, and the year after — without you paying for each visitor. This compounding nature of SEO is what makes it the highest-ROI digital marketing channel for most businesses over a 2–3 year horizon.

The businesses that get the best SEO ROI are those with: a reasonable conversion rate (above 1%), a meaningful order or lead value (above €50), and enough patience to let SEO compound over 6–18 months. If your numbers fit this profile, the ROI calculator above will show you just how significant the returns can be.

SEO ROI Questions Answered

How long does SEO take to show ROI?

Most businesses start seeing meaningful traffic improvements within 3–6 months of consistent SEO work. The breakeven point — where cumulative revenue from SEO exceeds the total investment — typically arrives between month 4 and month 9. After breakeven, every subsequent month is pure profit because the traffic cost is zero. The ROI then grows every month as rankings strengthen.

Is the calculator accurate?

The calculator uses your own numbers — your actual traffic, conversion rate, and order value — so the revenue figures are as accurate as the inputs you provide. The growth scenarios (50%, 100%, 200%, 300%) are illustrative — actual results depend on your competition, current domain authority, content quality, and the quality of SEO work. Use the calculator to understand the potential value at stake, not as a guarantee of specific results.

What conversion rate should I use?

If you have Google Analytics set up with goals, use your actual conversion rate. If you don't know it, here are typical benchmarks: eCommerce stores average 1–3%, local service businesses average 2–5% (contact form or phone call), SaaS free trial sign-ups average 3–7%. Start with 2% if you're unsure — this is a reasonable middle-ground estimate for most businesses.

What counts as a "conversion" for a service business?

For a service business, a conversion is any valuable action: a contact form submission, a phone call, a booking request, a quote request, or an email enquiry. For the average order value, use the average revenue you generate from a new customer — not just the first invoice, but the full customer value if they come back. A restaurant booking might be €40, a plumber enquiry might be worth €250, and an SEO client might be worth €3,500+.
