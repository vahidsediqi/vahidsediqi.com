FAQ

Common Questions

Does Google always use my meta title?
Not always. Google may rewrite your title if it considers it too long, keyword-stuffed, or not representative of the page content. Keeping your title within 50–60 characters and accurately describing the page gives you the best chance it will be used as-is.
Why does my description get truncated in search results?
Google truncates descriptions that exceed approximately 160 characters (or ~920 pixels wide). Write your most important information in the first 120–160 characters to ensure it's always visible.
Does the meta description affect my Google ranking?
Meta descriptions are not a direct ranking factor. However, a compelling description improves click-through rate (CTR), which signals engagement to Google and can indirectly improve your rankings over time.
What if no description is set on my page?
If no meta description is set, Google will automatically pull a snippet from your page content. While this sometimes works fine, you have no control over the message shown — which can hurt your CTR. Always write a custom description.
