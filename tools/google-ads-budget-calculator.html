Google Ads Tips

Make Every Pound Count

Start with exact & phrase match

Broad match burns budget fast. Start tight with exact and phrase match keywords, then expand once you see what converts.

Negative keywords are non-negotiable

Add negative keywords before you launch. "Free", "jobs", "DIY", and competitor names can waste 20–40% of your budget if ignored.

Use ad scheduling

Run ads only when your target audience is likely to convert. Most B2B businesses waste money running ads on weekends at full budget.

Adjust bids by device

If mobile converts poorly (common for high-ticket B2B), reduce mobile bids by 20–50% to shift spend toward desktop where intent is higher.

Match landing page to ad

A high Quality Score lowers your CPC. The #1 driver? Landing page relevance. Each ad group should link to a dedicated, matching page.

Give campaigns 30–60 days to learn

Google's Smart Bidding needs conversion data. Don't judge a campaign in week one — let it accumulate 30–50 conversions before optimising.

Common Questions

Google Ads Budget FAQ

There's no single right answer, but most small businesses start with €500–€2,000/month. The real question is: what's a conversion worth to you? If a new customer is worth €500, spending €200 to acquire one is profitable. Use this calculator to work backwards from your goals rather than picking an arbitrary number.
CPC (Cost Per Click) is what you pay each time someone clicks your ad. Google calculates it via an auction — your bid × Quality Score vs competitors. The average CPC varies hugely by industry: legal keywords can cost €15–€50 per click, while fashion might be €0.50–€1.50. This tool uses 2025 industry benchmarks as a starting point.
ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) is revenue divided by ad spend. A 3× ROAS means you earn €3 for every €1 spent. For eCommerce with ~50% margins, aim for at least 3–4×. For services (where cost of delivery is lower), 2× can still be profitable. A ROAS below 1× means you're losing money on ads.
Below €500/month in ad spend, managing it yourself can make sense. Above €1,000/month, a professional typically saves more than they cost — through better Quality Scores, negative keywords, and bid strategies. Badly managed accounts commonly waste 30–40% of their budget on irrelevant clicks.
Unlike SEO, Google Ads can drive traffic on day one. However, Smart Bidding strategies need 30–50 conversions before they optimise well. Expect the first 4–6 weeks to be a learning phase with higher CPCs and lower ROAS — this is normal. Budget for 60 days before making major decisions.
Google Ads gives you instant visibility at a cost-per-click. SEO builds organic rankings over 3–12 months but is free per click once ranked. The best strategy combines both: ads for immediate traffic and revenue while SEO compounds in the background. Use our SEO ROI Calculator to compare the long-term value.
