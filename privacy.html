Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 — How we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

1. Controller / Data Responsible

Waheedullah Sediqi (trading as Vahid Sediqi)
Pappelstraße 10, 04600 Altenburg, Germany
Email: contact@vahidsediqi.com

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact data — name and email address when you submit the contact form or send an email.
  • Communication data — content of messages you send us.
  • Usage data — IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time of visit, collected automatically via server logs and analytics.

We do not collect payment data. Any payments are processed via third-party platforms (e.g. Stripe, PayPal) under their own privacy policies.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis

  • Responding to enquiries — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract performance / pre-contractual measures).
  • Website analytics — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the website).
  • Legal obligations — Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

4. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for as long as necessary to fulfil the enquiry and for a maximum of 3 years thereafter, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Server logs are deleted after 30 days.

5. Third-Party Services

  • GitHub Pages / Hosting — this website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Server logs are processed by GitHub under their Privacy Statement.
  • Font Awesome — icons loaded via CDN (Cloudflare). May log your IP address.
  • Google Fonts — fonts may be loaded from Google servers. Google may log your IP address.
  • Calendly — if you book a call, you are redirected to Calendly under their own privacy policy.

6. Cookies

This website uses only technically necessary cookies (e.g. session state). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies. No cookie banner is required.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (Art. 15)
  • Rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") (Art. 17)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20)
  • Object to processing (Art. 21)
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@vahidsediqi.com. The competent supervisory authority in Germany is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz Thüringen.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at vahidsediqi.com/privacy. Material changes will be communicated by updating the date at the top of this page.
